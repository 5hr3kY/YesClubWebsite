Our Mission

Yes! Club USA is a student-led organization dedicated to making a positive impact in our local community of Murrieta and Temecula. Our mission is to empower students to become compassionate leaders through service, fostering a culture of giving back and creating meaningful change in the lives of those we serve.

We showcase to the Youth the importance of helping the community and disadvantaged!

Our Core Values

Youth

We believe in the power of empathy and kindness to create a more caring and inclusive community. We strive to understand and support those in need, fostering a culture of compassion in all our actions.

Empowerment

We are committed to developing the leadership potential of our members, empowering them to take initiative, inspire others, and make a positive impact in their communities. We believe that everyone has the capacity to lead and create change.

Service

We are dedicated to serving our local community through meaningful volunteer work and impactful projects. We believe that service is a powerful way to give back, build connections, and create lasting change in the lives of those we serve.
