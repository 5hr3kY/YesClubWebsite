Our Mission

Yes! Club USA is a student-led organization dedicated to making a positive impact in our local community of Murrieta and Temecula. Our mission is to empower students to become compassionate leaders through service, fostering a culture of giving back and creating meaningful change in the lives of those we serve.

We showcase to the youth the importance of helping the community and disadvantaged!

Our Core Values

Youth

We believe in the power of the youth to be able to make and influence a positive difference in the world!

Empowerment

We are committed to developing the leadership potential of our members, empowering them to take initiative, inspire others, and make a positive impact in their communities. We believe that everyone has the capacity to lead and create change.

Service

We are dedicated to serving our local community through meaningful volunteer work and impactful projects. We believe that service is a powerful way to give back, build connections, and create lasting change in the lives of those we serve.

Events

September: Child Cancer Awareness

we raise awareness about child cancer by folding and distributing paper origami to honor and support children fighting cancer. This initiative may also include fundraising or hospital visits.

October: Breast Cancer Awareness

Our members participated in or helped organize a community race to support breast cancer awareness. Funds raised may go toward Michelle's Place Cancer Resource Center. Members made softened pillows that will be donated to hospitals for cancer patients.

November: Turkey Walk & Share the Harvest

We host a week-long Thanksgiving charity walk. Participants will walk 7,000 steps per day for 7 days and donate $7 to help purchase turkeys. These turkeys will be donated to the Share the Harvest program at Murrieta Mesa High School, providing warm Thanksgiving meals to families in need.

December: Christmas Caroling

Our members spread holiday cheer by singing Christmas carols at a local senior center in Temecula. This event fosters intergenerational connections and brings joy to the elderly during the holiday season.

January: Chinese New Year Gala

We volunteer at Murrieta Mesa High School's Chinese Culture Club’s Chinese New Year Gala and selling Asian snacks there as part of the fundraiser.

February + March: Mexico Refugee Donations

All the clubs will collect and donate rice and beans to support refugee camps in the border of Mexico. We all drive together to bring the donations to border angels who then distribute the donations.

March + April: Autism Awareness Month

The clubs will collaborate with local businesses to create a discount card, encouraging community support while raising awareness about autism. Funds will be donated to families with autistic kids to show the community support.

April + May: Community Service Day

A special event to recognize and appreciate club members and advisors for their dedication throughout the year. This gathering will include food, awards, and a recap of the club’s impact.

May + June: Youth Summer Camp Training & Summer Camp

Volunteers will undergo training to prepare for the club’s annual summer camp. The camp itself will provide kids with fun and educational activities while fostering leadership among volunteers.

June: HOBY SoCal Leadership Conference

A representative from the Yes Club will be selected to attend a two-day leadership conference at the University of San Diego at the end of June.
